CalorStride support

For questions, defects or privacy requests, contact support@calorstride.com.

Reporting a problem

Include your app version and build number, iOS version, steps to reproduce, expected behavior and actual behavior. Remove private information from screenshots.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the initial alpha.

Do I need Apple Health?

No. Manual tracking is available without granting Health access.

Why are reminders not arriving?

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → CalorStride and the app’s reminder preferences. Focus settings can affect presentation.

Can I reinstall the app?

Do not uninstall to troubleshoot without first protecting your records. Removing the app may delete locally stored data. Contact support before attempting recovery.

Is this medical advice?

No. CalorStride is intended for general wellness, not diagnosis or treatment.
