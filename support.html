Reporting a problem

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • CalorStride version and build number.
  • iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Steps required to reproduce the issue.
  • Expected behavior and actual behavior.
  • Relevant screenshots, if available.

Please remove sensitive personal information from screenshots and attachments before submitting them.

Frequently asked questions

Is CalorStride free?

CalorStride is designed to remain free, without advertising, subscriptions, in-app purchases or paid feature restrictions.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the initial alpha.

Do I need Apple Health?

No. Manual tracking is available without granting Apple Health access.

Why are reminders not arriving?

Check your iPhone notification permissions, CalorStride reminder preferences and Focus settings. Scheduled notification delivery remains under validation during development.

Can I reinstall the application?

Contact support before uninstalling CalorStride during alpha testing. Removing the application may delete locally stored records.

Does CalorStride provide medical advice?

No. CalorStride is intended for general wellness and informational purposes, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

When will I receive a response?

CalorStride is independently developed. Support requests are reviewed as availability permits, and response times may vary.
