Alpha & beta testing

CalorStride is preparing for invitation-only testing through Apple TestFlight. Testing helps identify defects, improve usability and validate application performance before public release.

Internal alpha

In preparation. Initial testing is focused on application stability, functionality and data integrity.

External beta

Planned. External testing will begin after internal validation and applicable Apple review requirements.

Public release

Planned following successful testing and App Store review.

External testing invitations are not currently open.

For invited testers

  1. Install Apple TestFlight on your supported iPhone.
  2. Accept your CalorStride testing invitation.
  3. Install the available CalorStride build.
  4. Use the application and report any unexpected behavior.
  5. Install updated builds when available.

Download Apple TestFlight

What to expect

Pre-release software may contain defects, incomplete features or unexpected behavior. Application functionality may change between builds.

Do not rely on pre-release software as your sole record of important health or wellness information.

Submitting feedback

Testers can submit feedback directly through TestFlight or contact feedback@calorstride.com.

When reporting a defect, include the application version, iOS version, steps to reproduce and expected results.

Do not include sensitive health information or account credentials in feedback submissions.

Testing assistance

For installation or technical assistance, contact support@calorstride.com.

For privacy-related questions, contact privacy@calorstride.com or review our privacy policy.

Testing costs

Participation in CalorStride testing is free. No subscription, purchase or payment is required.
