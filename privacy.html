Privacy policy

Version 1.1 · November 23, 2026

Applies to the CalorStride website and planned initial application alpha.

Overview

CalorStride is an independently developed nutrition, fitness and general wellness application. Privacy and personal control are central to its design.

This policy describes the application's intended initial data-handling practices and the current website and email services. Application-specific practices will be verified before external beta distribution.

Information stored in the application

CalorStride is designed to store food entries, nutrition targets, personal food items, saved meals, workout records, progress measurements and preferences locally on your device.

The initial alpha does not require a user account or use a developer-hosted database for these records.

Apple Health

With your permission, CalorStride may access supported Apple Health information to display fitness and wellness measurements.

Apple Health access is optional. Permissions can be reviewed or revoked through iOS Settings or the Apple Health application.

CalorStride does not sell Apple Health information or use it for advertising, marketing or data brokerage.

Notifications

Optional reminders are designed to operate through iOS local notifications. Notification preferences can be managed within the application and through iOS Settings.

Artificial intelligence

The initial alpha does not enable direct external AI API access. Any future feature involving the transmission of personal information to an external AI provider will require a separate privacy review and policy update before activation.

Advertising and tracking

CalorStride's initial design does not include advertising, advertising trackers or the sale of personal information.

The official website does not incorporate developer-installed analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Website hosting and domain services

The official CalorStride website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Cloudflare provides domain and DNS services.

These service providers may process technical information necessary to operate their services, including network requests, IP addresses and security-related information where applicable.

Support and email communications

When you voluntarily contact CalorStride, your email address, message content and any attachments you provide may be processed to respond to your inquiry, investigate defects or address privacy requests.

Cloudflare Email Routing forwards incoming messages addressed to CalorStride email accounts to a Gmail inbox managed by the developer. Cloudflare and Google may process these communications as necessary to provide email delivery and storage services.

Replies may originate from a different email address while branded outgoing email services are being configured.

Please do not submit passwords, sensitive health records or unnecessary personal information through email or TestFlight feedback.

Third-party services

Apple may process application installation information, diagnostics and TestFlight feedback according to its own privacy practices.

Relevant service provider privacy policies include:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored application records remain on your device until removed through available application controls or deletion of the application. Device backups may retain additional copies depending on your iOS settings.

Revoking Apple Health permissions does not necessarily remove information previously stored by an application.

Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to resolve inquiries, investigate issues or comply with applicable obligations. You may request deletion of your correspondence by contacting the privacy email address below.

Security

The CalorStride website uses HTTPS. However, no method of electronic storage or communication can guarantee absolute security.

Users should protect their devices with appropriate security settings and keep their operating systems updated.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as CalorStride's features, infrastructure and data-handling practices evolve. The current version will remain available on this website.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning personal information, email privacy@calorstride.com.

For general application support, email support@calorstride.com.
