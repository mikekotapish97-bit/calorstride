Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · Applies to the initial CalorStride alpha.

CalorStride is independently developed for general fitness and nutrition tracking. This policy explains how the initial alpha handles information.

Information stored in the app

Food entries, nutrition targets, saved meals, workout records, progress measurements and preferences are stored locally on your device. The initial alpha does not require an account. CalorStride does not operate a developer-hosted database for these records.

Apple Health

With your permission, CalorStride accesses supported Apple Health information to display fitness and wellness measurements. Health access is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings or the Health app. Health information is not sold or used for advertising, marketing or data brokerage.

Notifications

Optional reminders use iOS local notifications. You can change reminder preferences in the app and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

AI features

The initial alpha does not enable direct external AI API access. Any future feature that sends personal information to an external service will require a separate review and an updated privacy policy before activation.

Sharing and service providers

CalorStride does not sell personal information or use advertising trackers. Apple may process installation, diagnostic and TestFlight feedback information under its own privacy terms. Device backups may be managed by iOS according to your settings. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses. See Apple’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Support communications

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to provide may be used to respond and investigate issues. Please do not include sensitive health records or credentials.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until deleted through available app controls or removal of the app. Device backups may retain copies. You can revoke Health access independently; revoking access does not necessarily remove previously stored information. Contact support to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable obligations.

Security and changes

No storage or communication method is completely secure. Protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS updated. This policy will be revised as features and data handling change.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: privacy@calorstride.com.
