Release notes

Follow CalorStride's development, feature updates and release milestones.

v0.2.3a — In preparation

The upcoming development milestone focuses on improved nutrition tracking and preparation for internal TestFlight distribution.

  • Personal food library and food favorites.
  • Reusable saved meals.
  • Expanded nutrition and macronutrient tracking.
  • Historical nutrition record navigation.
  • Improved dashboard and personal targets.
  • CalorStride application branding.

Internal alpha distribution remains pending final application validation.

November 2026 — Infrastructure update

  • Official calorstride.com domain established.
  • Secure HTTPS website enabled and validated.
  • Official support, privacy, feedback and contact email addresses established.
  • Incoming email delivery successfully validated.
  • App Store Connect and internal TestFlight preparations underway.

Known limitations

CalorStride remains under active development. Current limitations include:

  • Workout entry is currently manual.
  • Scheduled notification delivery remains under validation.
  • Expanded historical Apple Health synchronization is not yet available.
  • Public food database search and barcode scanning are planned features.
