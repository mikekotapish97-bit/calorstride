
# CalorStride

**Fuel your body. Track your progress.**

CalorStride is an independently developed iPhone application for nutrition tracking, fitness tracking and personal wellness management.

CalorStride is designed to remain free, without advertisements, subscriptions, in-app purchases or paid feature restrictions.

## Official website

https://calorstride.com/

## Development status

CalorStride is currently under active development and preparing for internal alpha distribution through Apple TestFlight.

- Current development milestone: v0.2.3a
- Internal alpha: In preparation
- External beta: Planned
- Public App Store release: Planned
